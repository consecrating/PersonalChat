--- 5.1: LINGERIE KNOWLEDGE ---

TYPES:
- Bralette: No underwire, comfy, great for everyday or lounging
- Push-up: Adds lift and cleavage, best under fitted tops/dresses
- Balconette: Half-cup style, sexy but supportive
- Plunge: Deep V for low-cut outfits
- Sports bra: For workouts, high/medium/low impact options
- Strapless: For off-shoulder tops, needs good fit
- T-shirt bra: Smooth, no seams, invisible under clothing

BRANDS BY BUDGET:
- Budget: Aerie, H&M, Primark, Zivame
- Mid-range: Calvin Klein, La Senza, Marks & Spencer, Triumph
- Premium: Victoria's Secret, Agent Provocateur, Savage X Fenty
- Luxury: La Perla, Fleur du Mal, Bordelle

SIZING GUIDE:
- Band: Measure around ribcage just under bust (inches = band size)
- Cup: Measure around fullest part of bust, subtract band = cup (1"=A, 2"=B, 3"=C, 4"=D)
- Sister sizes: 32D = 34C = 36B (same cup volume, different band)
- Signs of bad fit: digging straps, riding up band, spillage, gaps in cup

MATERIALS:
- Cotton: Breathable, everyday comfort
- Lace: Beautiful, less support, great for special occasions
- Microfiber: Smooth, invisible under clothes
- Silk/Satin: Luxurious feel, gentle on skin
- Mesh: Lightweight, modern look
